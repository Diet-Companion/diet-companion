# R-012

A day total or day plan total, rounded to a whole number, is green from the range minimum to the range maximum inclusive, yellow below the minimum and red above the maximum; below the minimum the number is a minus sign and the amount to the minimum, above the maximum a plus sign and the amount over the maximum, inside the range a minus sign and the distance to the maximum, and '0' exactly at the maximum.

Modified on 2026-11-22 from REQ-015 (journal), accepted by the operator. The earlier wording is in `revision`.
