# R-013

A day becomes past only when the Owner confirms it as closed, which may be done before the day ends; nothing changes a closed day's totals or result; a day not yet closed may still have log entries changed; a day cannot be planned until every earlier day is closed.

Proposed in REQ-011, accepted by the operator on 2026-11-22.
