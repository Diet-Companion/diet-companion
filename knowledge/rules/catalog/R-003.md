# R-003

A product with an average piece weight is counted by the piece. Two selectable products may not have the same name and shop, whatever their brand; names and shops are compared ignoring letter case and leading, trailing or repeated spaces. Adding, editing or restoring a product that would make such a pair is refused. The earlier versions R-004 keeps and soft-deleted products (R-006) are not selectable.

Modified on 2026-11-22 from REQ-002 (catalog), accepted by the operator. The earlier wording is in `revision`.
