# REQ-007: Catalog add time and size

**Actor:** Owner. **Need:** Adding a product or saving a dish takes about 5 minutes, and the catalog stays usable at about 100 products and 50 dishes. **Value:** Keeping the catalog does not become a chore.

## Scope

In:
- Typical time from the start of adding a product, or of creating a dish, to its being saved: 5 minutes. It is not a hard maximum. (INT-001#T163/T164, INT-001#T169/T170, INT-001#T317/T318, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#6 comment 7076475608)
- Catalog size of about 100 products and 50 dishes is a size estimate, not a limit. (INT-001#T181/T182, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#6 comment 7076484011)
- Internet connection is assumed always available at this stage. (INT-001#T173/T174)

Out:
- Offline use of the catalog. (INT-001#T173/T174)

## Acceptance criteria

### REQ-007/AC-001 (positive)

Key: product / no target / add_duration

- **Given** A normally filled catalog and an internet connection
- **When** Owner adds a product from a label photo or by lookup
- **Then** The typical time from start to saved is about 5 minutes
- **Then** No pass/fail limit applies; how it is checked is undecided (Q-015)

Cites: INT-001#T163/T164, INT-001#T317/T318, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#6 comment 7076475608, Q-015

### REQ-007/AC-002 (positive)

Key: dish / no target / save_duration

- **Given** The products of the dish exist in the catalog
- **When** Owner creates and saves a dish
- **Then** The typical time from start to saved is about 5 minutes
- **Then** No pass/fail limit applies; how it is checked is undecided (Q-015)

Cites: INT-001#T169/T170, INT-001#T317/T318, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#6 comment 7076475608, Q-015

### REQ-007/AC-003 (boundary)

Key: product / no target / catalog_size

- **Given** The catalog holds 100 products and 50 dishes
- **When** Owner adds a product or saves a dish
- **Then** The typical time of AC-001 and AC-002 still holds

Cites: INT-001#T181/T182, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#6 comment 7076484011, Q-015

### REQ-007/AC-004 (boundary)

Key: product / no target / catalog_size

- **Given** The catalog holds 100 products
- **When** Owner saves a 101st product
- **Then** The product is saved
- **Then** No size-limit error is shown

Cites: Diet-Companion/diet-companion#6 comment 7076484011
