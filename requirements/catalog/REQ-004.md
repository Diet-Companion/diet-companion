# REQ-004: Add a product by online lookup

**Actor:** Owner. **Need:** Give a product name and have the app look up values online and save the product. **Value:** Replaces manual lookups in chat.

## Scope

In:
- Name entered, online search, automatic save without review, source = online, default shop 'любой' (any). (INT-001#T137/T138, INT-001#T141/T142, INT-001#T145/T146, INT-001#T305/T306)
- When the lookup finds nothing or lacks calories, protein or fat, the app asks the Owner to type the missing values, then saves with source 'online'. (Diet-Companion/diet-companion#6 comment 7069241935)

Out:
- Review or confirmation of values that were found online. (INT-001#T141/T142)

## Acceptance criteria

### REQ-004/AC-001 (positive)

Key: product / no target / add_by_lookup

- **Given** No label; the Owner enters the name 'pear'; the online search returns calories, protein and fat
- **When** Owner submits the name
- **Then** The product is saved with the found values and source 'online'
- **Then** The Owner is not asked to review the values

Cites: INT-001#T137/T138, INT-001#T141/T142, INT-001#T145/T146

### REQ-004/AC-002 (positive)

Key: product / no target / default_shop

- **Given** A product is added by online lookup
- **When** The product is saved
- **Then** Its shop is 'любой'

Cites: INT-001#T305/T306

### REQ-004/AC-003 (positive)

Key: weekly_shopping_plan / no target / any_shop_group

- **Given** A product with shop 'любой' is in the weekly shopping plan, which is grouped by shop
- **When** Owner opens the weekly shopping plan
- **Then** The product appears in a separate 'любой' group, not under a named shop

Cites: INT-001#T309/T310

### REQ-004/AC-004 (negative)

Key: product / no target / lookup_not_found

- **Given** The online search for the name 'pear' finds nothing
- **When** Owner submits the name
- **Then** The app asks the Owner to type calories, protein and fat
- **Then** The product is saved with source 'online' after the Owner supplies them

Cites: Diet-Companion/diet-companion#6 comment 7069241935

### REQ-004/AC-005 (negative)

Key: product / no target / lookup_missing_required_value

- **Given** The online search finds calories and fat but no protein
- **When** Owner submits the name
- **Then** The app asks the Owner for protein only
- **Then** The product is saved with source 'online' after protein is supplied

Cites: Diet-Companion/diet-companion#6 comment 7069241935, INT-001#T9/T10

### REQ-004/AC-006 (negative)

Key: product / no target / lookup_missing_required_value

- **Given** The online search lacks protein and the Owner has not supplied it
- **When** Owner leaves without supplying protein
- **Then** The product is not saved

Cites: INT-001#T9/T10, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#6 comment 7069241935
