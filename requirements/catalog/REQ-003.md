# REQ-003: Add a product from a label photo

**Actor:** Owner. **Need:** Photograph a label, upload it, and have the product recorded without a confirmation step. **Value:** Fast catalog growth without typing values.

## Scope

In:
- Photo upload, recognition, automatic save, source = label. (INT-001#T117/T118, INT-001#T145/T146)
- The app asks only for a missing name, shop, calories, protein or fat. (INT-001#T125/T126, INT-001#T129/T130, INT-001#T133/T134)
- The photo is deleted after the product is saved. (INT-001#T177/T178)

Out:
- Review or confirmation of recognised values. (INT-001#T121/T122)

## Acceptance criteria

### REQ-003/AC-001 (positive)

Key: label / no target / add_from_photo

- **Given** A label photo shows name, shop (for example Lidl), calories, protein and fat
- **When** Owner uploads the photo
- **Then** The product is saved with source 'label'
- **Then** The Owner is not asked to confirm anything

Cites: INT-001#T117/T118, INT-001#T121/T122, INT-001#T145/T146

### REQ-003/AC-002 (negative)

Key: product / no target / shop_from_photo

- **Given** A label photo with no shop mentioned
- **When** Owner uploads the photo
- **Then** The app asks the Owner for the shop
- **Then** The product is saved after the Owner supplies it

Cites: INT-001#T125/T126

### REQ-003/AC-003 (negative)

Key: product / no target / required_value_unrecognized

- **Given** A label photo where protein cannot be recognised
- **When** Owner uploads the photo
- **Then** The app asks the Owner for protein
- **Then** The product is not saved until it is supplied

Cites: INT-001#T129/T130, INT-001#T9/T10

### REQ-003/AC-004 (negative)

Key: product / no target / name_from_photo

- **Given** A label photo that gives no product name
- **When** Owner uploads the photo
- **Then** The app asks the Owner for the name

Cites: INT-001#T133/T134

### REQ-003/AC-005 (positive)

Key: label / no target / photo_after_save

- **Given** A product was saved from a label photo
- **When** The product is written to the catalog
- **Then** The uploaded photo is deleted

Cites: INT-001#T177/T178
