# REQ-006: Save, use, edit and soft-delete dishes

**Actor:** Owner. **Need:** Save a dish as a combination of products with amounts and plan or log it in one step. **Value:** Repeat dishes are planned and logged in one step.

## Scope

In:
- A dish needs a name and at least one product to be saved; it lists products with weights; a product counted by the piece may be given in pieces, weight = pieces x average piece weight; pieces are allowed only for products with an average piece weight. (INT-001#T52, INT-001#T54, INT-001#T58, INT-001#T59/T60, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#6 comment 7076464547)
- A dish is planned and logged as a whole portion; a log entry that differs lists all products with that time's amounts; the catalog dish is not changed. (INT-001#T63/T64, INT-001#T67/T68, INT-001#T71/T72)
- A different amount in a day plan creates a new dish. Changing a dish's composition or amounts creates a new dish; the old one is unchanged; past entries keep the old one; next planning uses the new one. (INT-001#T285/T286, INT-001#T99/T100, INT-001#T105/T106, INT-001#T289/T290)
- When a product in a saved dish gets new nutrient values, the dish keeps pointing at the old product. (Diet-Companion/diet-companion#6 comment 7076464547)
- Dish soft delete: visible only in the log. The Owner can restore a soft-deleted dish. (INT-001#T109/T110, INT-001#T113/T114, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#6 comment 7076475608, A-010)
- Two dishes may have the same name. (Diet-Companion/diet-companion#6 comment 7076475608)

Out:
- None.
