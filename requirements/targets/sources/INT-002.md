## Decisions

- The daily target ranges can be changed over time (INT-002#T3/T4).
- A changed range applies from the day the change is made (INT-002#T7/T8).
- Days before a change are compared with the range that applied before the change (INT-002#T11/T12).
- The same ranges apply to every day; there are no day-specific ranges (INT-002#T15/T16).
- Status colour: red above the maximum, yellow below the minimum, green inside the range (INT-002#T23/T24).
- Outside the range, the difference is shown from the crossed limit: from the minimum when below, from the maximum when above (INT-002#T29/T30).
- Inside the range, no difference or extra number is shown (INT-002#T33/T34).
- There's no separate near-limit state (INT-002#T37/T38).
- The day's compared totals are the sum of the log entries (INT-002#T41/T42).
- The day plan's totals are compared with the ranges and shown the same way as the actual day (INT-002#T47/T48).
- The comparison of logged totals is available at any time (INT-002#T51/T52).
- A partly logged day gets no special treatment (INT-002#T55/T56).
- Only calories, protein and fat have ranges now; a salt range may come later and is outside this scope (INT-002#T59/T60).
- Editing a product's nutrient values doesn't change past days' totals or results (INT-002#T63/T64).
- Nothing changes a past day's totals or result (INT-002#T67/T68).
- A day becomes past only when the owner confirms it as closed (INT-002#T71/T72).
- The app reminds the owner to close an unclosed day (INT-002#T75/T76).
- The reminder comes when the owner goes to plan the next day (INT-002#T79/T80).
- Planning the next day is blocked until the previous day is closed (INT-002#T83/T84).
- A range's minimum can't be higher than its maximum (INT-002#T87/T88).
- Every range has both a minimum and a maximum (INT-002#T91/T92).
- The ranges for calories, protein and fat are always set (INT-002#T95/T96).
- On first use, the owner sets all three ranges before anything else (INT-002#T99/T100).
- Meals have no target ranges of their own (INT-002#T103/T104).
- Totals are shown for each meal as well as for the day, with no comparison for meal totals (INT-002#T107/T108).
- Totals are shown with no decimals (INT-002#T111/T112).
- The range check uses the total as shown, with no decimals (INT-002#T115/T116).
- Totals are rounded to the nearest whole number (INT-002#T119/T120).
- A halfway value is rounded by the implementation language's or math library's standard rounding (INT-002#T135/T136).
- A product can be planned or logged only after it has been added to the catalog with its values (INT-002#T125/T126).
- Nothing more is out of scope beyond the later salt range (INT-002#T129/T130).
