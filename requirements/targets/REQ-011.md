# REQ-011: Close a day and be reminded to close open days

**Actor:** Owner. **Need:** Confirm a day as closed so its totals and result are fixed, and be reminded to close open days when planning, without being blocked from planning today or the next day. **Value:** Keeps past results trustworthy for the diet record without stopping planning.

## Scope

In:
- Closing a day (including today before it ends, and a day with no log entries), editing open past days, the reminder to close open days, planning allowed whether or not earlier days are closed (R-013). (INT-002#T71/T72, INT-002#T79/T80, R-013, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#12 comment 7077180098, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#12 comment 7077225554, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#19)

Out:
- Reopening a closed day: nothing may change a past day. Blocking planning because earlier days are open (removed by R-013). Planning more than 1 day ahead (journal REQ-013). (INT-002#T67/T68, R-013, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#19)

## Acceptance criteria

### REQ-011/AC-001 (positive)

Key: day / no target / closing

- **Given** Day D is open and has log entries
- **When** Owner confirms day D as closed
- **Then** Day D becomes a past day
- **Then** Its totals and result against the ranges are fixed

Cites: INT-002#T71/T72

### REQ-011/AC-002 (negative)

Key: day / no target / closing

- **Given** Day D is open and the date has moved to D+1
- **Given** Owner has not confirmed day D as closed
- **When** Owner views day D
- **Then** Day D is not a past day; it stays open

Cites: INT-002#T71/T72

### REQ-011/AC-003 (negative)

Key: day total / no target / past day immutability

- **Given** Day D is closed with a calories total of 1300
- **When** Owner tries to change a log entry of day D
- **Then** The change is not applied
- **Then** The totals and the result of day D remain unchanged, 1300

Cites: INT-002#T67/T68

### REQ-011/AC-004 (positive)

Key: day / no target / close reminder timing

- **Given** Day D is still open
- **Given** Day D+1 is the day to be planned
- **When** Owner goes to plan day D+1
- **Then** The app reminds the Owner to close day D

Cites: INT-002#T79/T80, R-013, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#19

### REQ-011/AC-005 (negative)

Key: day plan / no target / blocked by unclosed day

- **Given** Day D is open and the reminder is shown
- **When** Owner tries to build the plan for day D+1 without closing day D
- **Then** Planning day D+1 is blocked until day D is closed

Cites: INT-002#T83/T84, A-005

### REQ-011/AC-006 (positive)

- **Then** No reminder is shown
- **Then** Planning day D+1 is allowed

Cites: INT-002#T83/T84, R-013
