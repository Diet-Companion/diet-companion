# REQ-011: Close a day and block planning until it is closed

**Actor:** Owner. **Need:** Confirm a day as closed so its totals and result are fixed, and be reminded to close it before planning the next day. **Value:** Keeps past results trustworthy for the diet record.

## Scope

In:
- Closing a day (including today before it ends, and a day with no log entries), editing open past days, the reminder, and blocking planning while any earlier day is open. (INT-002#T71/T72, INT-002#T79/T80, INT-002#T83/T84, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#12 comment 7077180098, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#12 comment 7077225554)

Out:
- Reopening a closed day: nothing may change a past day. (INT-002#T67/T68)

## Acceptance criteria

### REQ-011/AC-001 (positive)

Key: day / no target / closing

- **Given** Day D is open and has log entries
- **When** Owner confirms day D as closed
- **Then** Day D becomes a past day
- **Then** Its totals and result against the ranges are fixed

Cites: INT-002#T71/T72

### REQ-011/AC-002 (negative)

Key: day / no target / closing

- **Given** Day D is open and the date has moved to D+1
- **Given** Owner has not confirmed day D as closed
- **When** Owner views day D
- **Then** Day D is not a past day; it stays open

Cites: INT-002#T71/T72

### REQ-011/AC-003 (negative)

Key: day total / no target / past day immutability

- **Given** Day D is closed with a calories total of 1300
- **When** Owner tries to change a log entry of day D
- **Then** The change is not applied
- **Then** The totals and the result of day D remain unchanged, 1300

Cites: INT-002#T67/T68

### REQ-011/AC-004 (positive)

Key: day / no target / close reminder timing

- **Given** Day D is still open
- **Given** Day D+1 is the day to be planned
- **When** Owner goes to plan day D+1
- **Then** The app reminds the Owner to close day D

Cites: INT-002#T79/T80, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#12 comment 7077180098

### REQ-011/AC-005 (negative)

Key: day plan / no target / blocked by unclosed day

- **Given** Day D is open and the reminder is shown
- **When** Owner tries to build the plan for day D+1 without closing day D
- **Then** Planning day D+1 is blocked until day D is closed

Cites: INT-002#T83/T84, A-005

### REQ-011/AC-006 (positive)

Key: day plan / no target / allowed when earlier days closed

- **Given** Every day before D+1 has been confirmed as closed
- **When** Owner goes to plan day D+1
- **Then** No reminder is shown
- **Then** Planning day D+1 is allowed

Cites: INT-002#T83/T84, A-005

### REQ-011/AC-007 (positive)

Key: day / no target / edits before closing

- **Given** Day D-2 is a date before today and has not been confirmed as closed
- **When** Owner adds, changes or removes a log entry of day D-2
- **Then** The change is applied
- **Then** Day D-2's totals and result are recalculated and stay open to change until closed

Cites: A-003, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#12 comment 7077180098

### REQ-011/AC-008 (positive)

Key: day / no target / closing before day end

- **Given** Today is day D and has not ended
- **When** Owner confirms day D as closed
- **Then** Day D becomes a past day with fixed totals and result

Cites: A-007, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#12 comment 7077180098

### REQ-011/AC-009 (negative)

Key: day plan / no target / which earlier days block planning

- **Given** Day D-2 is open
- **Given** Day D-1 is confirmed as closed
- **When** Owner tries to build the plan for day D
- **Then** The app reminds the Owner to close day D-2
- **Then** Planning day D is blocked until day D-2 is closed

Cites: A-005, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#12 comment 7077180098

### REQ-011/AC-010 (boundary)

Key: day / no target / closing an empty day

- **Given** Day D is open and has no log entries
- **When** Owner confirms day D as closed
- **Then** Day D becomes a past day with fixed totals and result

Cites: A-009, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#12 comment 7077225554

### REQ-011/AC-011 (boundary)

Key: day plan / no target / days before first use

- **Given** The first day of use is day F
- **Given** Owner has confirmed no day as closed
- **When** Owner goes to plan day F
- **Then** No reminder is shown for any date before F
- **Then** Planning day F is allowed

Cites: A-008, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#12 comment 7077225554
