# REQ-010: Compare day totals and day plan with ranges

**Actor:** Owner. **Need:** See at any time whether the logged day and the day plan fit the ranges, with colour and a signed number: the amount to the minimum or over the maximum outside the range, and the distance to the maximum inside it. **Value:** Shows at a glance whether a day fits the diet, replacing manual comparison.

## Scope

In:
- Colour status, limits inside the range, signed number display per R-012, rounding, plan comparison, partly logged days, meal totals without comparison. (INT-002#T23/T24, INT-002#T119/T120, INT-002#T107/T108, R-012, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#19)

Out:
- Near-limit state; ranges per meal; changes to ranges, colours or rounding. (INT-002#T37/T38, INT-002#T103/T104, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#19)

## Acceptance criteria

### REQ-010/AC-001 (positive)

Key: day total / no target / status colour

- **Given** Calories range is 1800 to 2200
- **Given** The day's calories total is 1500
- **When** Owner views the day
- **Then** The calories total shows yellow
- **Then** The difference from the minimum is shown as an unsigned number: 300
