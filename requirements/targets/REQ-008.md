# REQ-008: Daily nutrient targets

**Actor:** Owner. **Need:** Daily target ranges for calories, protein and fat that tell whether a day's food fits the diet, applied to the day plan and the log. **Value:** Replaces the manual comparison done in chat; the only dietary rule is that each day's totals fit inside the ranges (no forbidden products).

## Scope

In:
- Ranges (minimum to maximum) for calories, protein and fat, changeable over time, the same on every day. (INT-002#T3/T4, INT-002#T15/T16, INT-002#T59/T60)
- Comparison of the logged day and the day plan with the ranges, with colours and differences. (INT-002#T23/T24, INT-002#T47/T48)
- Closing a day, which fixes its totals and result. (INT-002#T71/T72)

Out:
- Ranges for other nutrients; a salt range may come later. (INT-002#T59/T60)
- Ranges per meal; meal totals are shown without comparison. (INT-002#T103/T104, INT-002#T107/T108)
- No other exclusions were named. (INT-002#T129/T130)
