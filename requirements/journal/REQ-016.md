# REQ-016: View past days; open past days highlighted

**Actor:** Owner. **Need:** See any past day and notice past days that are still open. Closing a day is owned by REQ-011; this requirement keeps that a closed day can't be changed or reopened (AC-002), that a planned but unlogged day stays open (AC-003) and that closing a day leaves an earlier open day open (AC-006). **Value:** Unclosed days are not forgotten.

## Scope

In:
- Viewing any past day; open past days highlighted in the list or navigation of days; a closed day can't be changed or reopened; planned-but-unlogged days stay open (the plan-only case of REQ-011/AC-002); closing a day while an earlier day is open (kept per Q-010 a). (Diet-Companion/diet-companion#127, INT-003#T99/T100, INT-003#T103/T104, INT-003#T111/T112, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#16 comment 7077906037, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#127 comment 7188649999, INT-003#T97/T98, INT-003#T113/T114, REQ-011)

Out:
- Closing a day itself, the reminder to close open days, and planning while days are open: owned by REQ-011 and R-013. (Diet-Companion/diet-companion#127, REQ-011, R-013)

## Acceptance criteria

### REQ-016/AC-001 (boundary)

Key: Closed day / no target / past days visible

- **Given** A day 400 days ago exists
- **When** Owner opens that day
- **Then** The day is shown

Cites: INT-003#T111/T112

### REQ-016/AC-002 (negative)

Key: Closed day / no target / editability

- **Given** Day D is closed and has a mistake
- **When** Owner tries to change or reopen day D
- **Then** The change is not applied
- **Then** The day stays read-only

Cites: INT-003#T113/T114, R-013

### REQ-016/AC-003 (negative)

Key: Day plan / no target / day never logged

- **Given** Day D had a day plan and nothing was logged
- **Given** Day D has passed
- **When** Owner views day D
- **Then** Day D is still open
- **Then** It was not closed automatically

Cites: INT-003#T97/T98

### REQ-016/AC-004 (positive)

Key: Closed day / no target / open past day indication

- **Given** Day D is before today and not closed
- **When** Owner views the list or navigation of days
- **Then** Day D is highlighted as still open there

Cites: INT-003#T99/T100, A-011, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#16 comment 7077906037

### REQ-016/AC-005 (negative)

Key: Closed day / no target / open past day indication on closed day

- **Given** Day D is closed
- **When** Owner views the list or navigation of days
- **Then** No open-day highlight is shown for day D

Cites: INT-003#T99/T100, A-011

### REQ-016/AC-006 (positive)

Key: Closed day / no target / closing order

- **Given** Day D-1 is open
- **Given** Day D is open
- **When** Owner closes day D
- **Then** Day D is closed
- **Then** Day D-1 stays open

Cites: INT-003#T103/T104, R-013
