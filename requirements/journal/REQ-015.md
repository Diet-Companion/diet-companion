# REQ-015: No per-meal comparison or extra warnings in the journal

**Actor:** Owner. **Need:** See no plan-versus-actual difference per meal or per product, and no warning or block beyond the number and colour. The comparison with the ranges, and what the day total counts, are owned by REQ-010. **Value:** One requirement owns each behaviour, so it is built and tested once.

## Scope

In:
- No per-meal or per-product plan-vs-actual difference; no warning or block beyond the number and colour. (Diet-Companion/diet-companion#127, INT-003#T71/T72, INT-003#T161/T162, A-001)

Out:
- The day total counts the logged meals, not the plan: owned by REQ-010 (AC-007). (Diet-Companion/diet-companion#127, REQ-010, INT-003#T27/T28)
- Colour and signed number of a logged day total or a plan total against the ranges, including boundaries: owned by REQ-010 through R-012. (Diet-Companion/diet-companion#127, REQ-010, R-012)
- Per-meal or per-product plan-vs-actual comparison. (INT-003#T71/T72)
