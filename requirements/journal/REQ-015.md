# REQ-015: Compare logged and planned day totals with target ranges (signed numbers)

**Actor:** Owner. **Need:** See, per nutrient, one signed number and colour comparing the logged meals so far, and the day plan, with the daily target range. **Value:** Shows at a glance what is still needed or exceeded.

## Scope

In:
- Signed difference numbers and colours for the log (meals already logged) and the day plan, for calories (kcal), protein and fat; no other plan-vs-actual comparison; no other warnings. (INT-003#T39/T40, INT-003#T71/T72, INT-003#T161/T162, A-001)

Out:
- Per-meal or per-product plan-vs-actual comparison. (INT-003#T71/T72)

## Acceptance criteria

### REQ-015/AC-001 (positive)

Key: Day total / no target / display below minimum

- **Given** Protein range is 160-180 g
- **Given** Logged meals total 60 g protein
- **When** Owner views the day total
- **Then** Protein shows '-100g' in yellow

Cites: INT-003#T53/T54, INT-003#T65/T66

### REQ-015/AC-002 (positive)

Key: Day total / no target / display inside range

- **Given** Protein range is 160-180 g
- **Given** Logged meals total 170 g protein
- **When** Owner views the day total
- **Then** Protein shows '-10' (distance to maximum) in green

Cites: INT-003#T57/T58, INT-003#T67/T68

### REQ-015/AC-003 (positive)

Key: Day total / no target / display above maximum

- **Given** Protein range is 160-180 g
- **Given** Logged meals total 280 g protein
- **When** Owner views the day total
- **Then** Protein shows '+100g' in red

Cites: INT-003#T55/T56, INT-003#T69/T70

### REQ-015/AC-004 (boundary)

Key: Day total / no target / display at minimum

- **Given** Protein range is 160-180 g
- **Given** Logged meals total exactly 160 g protein
- **When** Owner views the day total
- **Then** Protein is inside the range and shows '-20' in green

Cites: R-012, INT-003#T43/T44

### REQ-015/AC-005 (boundary)

Key: Day total / no target / display at maximum

- **Given** Protein range is 160-180 g
- **Given** Logged meals total exactly 180 g protein
- **When** Owner views the day total
- **Then** Protein is inside the range and shows '0' in green

Cites: R-012, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#16 comment 7077906037

### REQ-015/AC-006 (positive)

Key: Day total / no target / comparison basis

- **Given** Only breakfast is logged, with 40 g protein
- **Given** The day plan holds 170 g protein
- **Given** Protein range is 160-180 g
- **When** Owner views the day total
- **Then** The number is computed from the 40 g logged, not from the plan: '-120g' in yellow

Cites: INT-003#T27/T28, INT-003#T39/T40

### REQ-015/AC-007 (positive)

Key: Day plan / no target / display below minimum

- **Given** The plan totals 60 g protein
- **When** Owner views the day plan
- **Then** Protein shows '-100g' in yellow

Cites: INT-003#T155/T156

### REQ-015/AC-008 (positive)

- **Then** Protein shows '-20' in green

### REQ-015/AC-010 (negative)

Key: Day plan / no target / plan-vs-actual comparison in log
