# REQ-015: Day total counts the log, with no per-meal comparison or extra warnings

**Actor:** Owner. **Need:** See that the journal day total counts what I logged and not the plan, that no plan-versus-actual difference is shown per meal or per product, and that no warning or block appears beyond the number and colour. The comparison with the ranges is owned by REQ-010. **Value:** One requirement owns each behaviour, so it is built and tested once.

## Scope

In:
- Day total in the journal is computed from the logged meals, not from the plan; no per-meal or per-product plan-vs-actual difference; no warning or block beyond the number and colour. The signed number has no unit (e.g. '-120'). (Diet-Companion/diet-companion#127, INT-003#T27/T28, INT-003#T71/T72, INT-003#T161/T162, A-001)

Out:
- Colour and signed number of a logged day total or a plan total against the ranges, including boundaries: owned by REQ-010 through R-012. (Diet-Companion/diet-companion#127, REQ-010, R-012)
- Per-meal or per-product plan-vs-actual comparison. (INT-003#T71/T72)

## Acceptance criteria

### REQ-015/AC-001 (positive, retired)

Key: Day total / no target / display below minimum

- **Given** Protein range is 160-180 g
- **Given** Logged meals total 60 g protein
- **When** Owner views the day total
- **Then** Protein shows '-100g' in yellow
