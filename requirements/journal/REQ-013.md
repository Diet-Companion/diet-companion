# REQ-013: Plan a day per meal, up to one day ahead

**Actor:** Owner. **Need:** Plan products and dishes for each of the five meals of today or the next day, without having to close earlier days first. **Value:** The Owner can plan the next day during the current day.

## Scope

In:
- Day plan with products and dishes per meal; amounts in whole grams or whole piece counts; planning today and the next day, never blocked by open earlier days; the reminder to close open days is kept. (Diet-Companion/diet-companion#16, INT-003#T83/T84, INT-003#T91/T92, INT-003#T131/T132, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#16 comment 7077906037)

Out:
- Planning more than 1 day ahead. (INT-003#T83/T84)

## Acceptance criteria

### REQ-013/AC-001 (positive)

Key: Day plan / no target / contents per meal

- **Given** Products and a dish 'omelette' exist in the catalog
- **Given** The Owner is planning tomorrow
- **When** Owner adds 'omelette' to breakfast and 100 g of 'rice' to lunch
- **Then** The day plan holds the whole dish at breakfast and 100 g rice at lunch

Cites: Diet-Companion/diet-companion#16, R-007

### REQ-013/AC-002 (positive)

Key: Day plan / no target / planning next day while today open

- **Given** Today is not closed
- **When** Owner plans the next day
- **Then** The plan is saved

Cites: INT-003#T87/T88, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#16 comment 7077906037

### REQ-013/AC-003 (positive)

Key: Day plan / no target / planning next day while earlier days open

- **Given** A day before today is not closed
- **Given** Today is not closed
- **When** Owner plans the next day
- **Then** The plan is saved
- **Then** Planning is not blocked

Cites: INT-003#T91/T92, R-013, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#16 comment 7077906037

### REQ-013/AC-004 (boundary)

Key: Day plan / no target / planning horizon

- **Given** Today is day D
- **When** Owner tries to plan day D+2
- **Then** The plan is not accepted
- **Then** Only days up to D+1 can be planned

Cites: INT-003#T83/T84, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#16 comment 7077906037

### REQ-013/AC-005 (positive)

Key: Day plan / no target / amount precision

- **Given** The Owner is planning a meal
- **When** Owner enters 150 g of a product
- **Then** The amount 150 g is accepted

Cites: INT-003#T137/T138

### REQ-013/AC-006 (negative)

Key: Day plan / no target / fractional amount

- **Given** The Owner is planning a meal
- **When** Owner enters 150.5 g of a product
- **Then** The amount is rejected and the plan is not changed

Cites: INT-003#T137/T138, A-008, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#16 comment 7077906037

### REQ-013/AC-007 (negative)

Key: Day plan / no target / closed day

- **Given** Day D is closed
- **When** Owner tries to change the plan of day D
- **Then** The change is not applied

Cites: R-013
