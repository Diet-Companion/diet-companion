# REQ-014: Log meals after eating

**Actor:** Owner. **Need:** Log each meal after eating it, by confirming the plan, adjusting the plan, or entering it from scratch. **Value:** Logging stays quick when the day goes to plan.

## Scope

In:
- Quick log by confirming the plan, offered only for a meal that has a plan; load plan as actual and modify; enter from scratch with dishes or products; no limits relative to the plan; whole-gram and whole-piece amounts. (INT-003#T13/T14, INT-003#T15/T16, INT-003#T17/T18, INT-003#T77/T78, INT-003#T137/T138, Diet-Companion/diet-companion#16 comment 7077921879)

Out:
- None.

## Acceptance criteria

### REQ-014/AC-001 (positive)

Key: Log entry / no target / quick log from plan

- **Given** Lunch in today's day plan holds 100 g rice and 150 g chicken
- **When** Owner confirms lunch went according to the plan
- **Then** Log entries for lunch hold 100 g rice and 150 g chicken

Cites: INT-003#T13/T14

### REQ-014/AC-002 (positive)

Key: Log entry / no target / start from plan

- **Given** Lunch in today's day plan holds 100 g rice
- **When** Owner loads the lunch plan as actual and changes rice to 80 g
- **Then** The lunch log holds 80 g rice
- **Then** The day plan still holds 100 g rice

Cites: INT-003#T15/T16

### REQ-014/AC-003 (positive)

Key: Log entry / no target / start from scratch

- **Given** Lunch has a plan
- **When** Owner enters lunch from scratch with 200 g of 'soup'
- **Then** The lunch log holds only 200 g soup

Cites: INT-003#T17/T18

### REQ-014/AC-004 (positive)

Key: Log entry / no target / from-scratch dish

- **Given** Dish 'omelette' is in the catalog
- **When** Owner selects 'omelette' from the catalog for dinner
- **Then** The log entry holds the whole dish portion

Cites: INT-003#T19/T20, R-007

### REQ-014/AC-005 (positive)

Key: Log entry / no target / dish not in catalog

- **Given** The eaten dish is not in the catalog
- **When** Owner logs each of its products separately
- **Then** One log entry per product with its amount is saved

Cites: INT-003#T21/T22

### REQ-014/AC-006 (negative)

Key: Log entry / no target / limits relative to plan

- **Given** Breakfast plan holds only oatmeal
- **When** Owner logs 50 g chocolate at breakfast
- **Then** The entry is saved
- **Then** No limit or warning is shown

Cites: INT-003#T77/T78, INT-003#T161/T162

### REQ-014/AC-007 (positive)

Key: Log entry / no target / meal of a moved item

- **Given** Apple is planned for brunch
- **When** Owner logs the apple at lunch
- **Then** The log entry is at lunch, not brunch

Cites: INT-003#T79/T80

### REQ-014/AC-008 (positive)

Key: Log entry / no target / nutrients of a count

- **Given** Egg has average piece weight 50 g in the catalog
- **When** Owner logs 2 eggs
- **Then** The entry weighs 100 g and its nutrients come from the catalog data of egg

Cites: INT-003#T133/T134, INT-003#T135/T136, R-007

### REQ-014/AC-009 (negative)

Key: Log entry / no target / pieces without piece weight

- **Given** A product has no average piece weight
- **When** Owner logs it in pieces
- **Then** The pieces amount is not accepted

Cites: R-007

### REQ-014/AC-010 (positive)

Key: Dish / no target / different amount eaten

- **Given** Dish 'omelette' has 100 g egg white and 2 eggs
- **When** Owner logs it with 150 g egg white and 2 eggs, each product in grams
- **Then** The log entry lists the products with 150 g egg white and 2 eggs
- **Then** The catalog dish keeps 100 g egg white

Cites: INT-003#T143/T144, REQ-006/AC-006, REQ-006/AC-007

### REQ-014/AC-011 (negative)

Key: Log entry / no target / closed day

- **Given** Day D is closed
- **When** Owner tries to add a log entry to day D
- **Then** The entry is not saved

Cites: R-013

### REQ-014/AC-012 (negative)

Key: Log entry / no target / quick log without plan

- **Given** Lunch has no plan
- **When** Owner chooses to confirm lunch as planned
- **Then** Nothing is logged; what the Owner sees is open (Q-005)

Cites: A-009, Q-005

### REQ-014/AC-013 (negative)

Key: Log entry / no target / quick log without plan

- **Given** Lunch has no plan
- **Given** Breakfast has a plan
- **When** Owner opens the meal list of the log
- **Then** The confirm-as-planned button is not shown for lunch
- **Then** The confirm-as-planned button is shown for breakfast

Cites: Diet-Companion/diet-companion#16 comment 7077921879, A-009, Q-005
