context:
  text: 'A dish is planned and logged as a whole portion (R-007, REQ-014/AC-004). The Owner may also log a dish with other
    amounts: each product in grams or pieces, and the catalog dish stays unchanged (REQ-014/AC-010, REQ-006/AC-007). A dish
    not in the catalog is logged as separate product entries (REQ-014/AC-005). A dish line may point at a product that was
    later replaced or soft-deleted (ADR-014 option a); a new entry accepts only selectable products (ADR-017/C-003), so a
    client that resends those product ids would be refused.'
  cites:
  - R-007
  - REQ-014/AC-004
  - REQ-014/AC-005
  - REQ-014/AC-010
  - REQ-006/AC-007
  - ADR-012/C-005
  - ADR-014/C-001
  - ADR-017/C-003
options:
- key: a
  summary: A server operation addLogDishProducts takes a meal, a selectable dish id and optional amount overrides by line
    position. It writes one product entry per dish line, with that line's exact product id and amount (or the override), and
    from_dish_id set to the dish. Whole-dish logging stays a dish entry from addLogEntry.
  satisfies:
  - REQ-014/AC-005
  - REQ-014/AC-010
  - REQ-014/AC-008
  - REQ-014/AC-009
  pros: The exact product ids come from the dish, so a dish with a replaced or deleted product can still be logged (R-009).
    All lines are saved in one transaction. The catalog dish is read only. from_dish_id tells the client the entries came
    from a dish.
  cons: One more operation. A client changing one amount sends the whole override list or a few positions.
  cost: One handler and one request schema. Build effort not stated (A-034).
  reversibility: two-way
  risks: A client could expect later edits of the dish to reach the entries. Mitigated by the constraint that from_dish_id
    never feeds totals or edits.
  constraints:
  - id: ADR-018/C-001
    statement: addLogDishProducts accepts only a selectable dish (entry.invalid, dish_not_found or dish_not_selectable) and
      reads its lines by the exact dish id. Each written entry keeps the line's exact product_id, even when that product is
      replaced or soft-deleted; no selectable check is made on carried lines.
    kind: interface
    check: review
  - id: ADR-018/C-002
    statement: Overrides are given by line position; a position not in the dish is refused (reason line_unknown), a position
      not listed keeps the dish amount. An override or a dish amount in pieces needs a piece weight on that exact product
      id (piece_without_weight), and every amount is a whole number above 0 (amount_invalid). Nothing is saved when any line
      is refused, and the response lists the lines by index.
    kind: interface
    check: review
  - id: ADR-018/C-003
    statement: 'The operation never writes to dishes or dish_lines (ADR-012/C-005). from_dish_id is informational: it is not
      used in totals, edits or removal, and each entry is changed or removed on its own.'
    kind: data
    check: review
  - id: ADR-018/C-004
    statement: The split is available for the log only. A plan holds a whole dish or product entries added one by one.
    kind: interface
    check: review
  affects:
  - CMP-002
  - CMP-001
  - IF-001
  consequences: The log screen offers 'log products separately' on a dish and calls one operation. Entries made this way are
    ordinary product entries.
  cites:
  - REQ-014/AC-010
  - ADR-012/C-005
  - ADR-014/C-001
  - A-034
- key: b
  summary: The client reads the dish with getDish and sends ordinary product entries with addLogEntry, one per line, with
    the amounts the Owner set. No server operation for dishes and no dish link on the entries.
  satisfies:
  - REQ-014/AC-005
  - REQ-014/AC-010
  pros: No new operation. The interface stays smaller.
  cons: Each line is a separate request, so a failure can leave a half-logged dish. A dish line whose product was replaced
    or deleted is refused by the selectable check, so such a dish cannot be logged by products. No record that entries came
    from a dish.
  cost: Client code only. Build effort not stated (A-034).
  reversibility: two-way
  risks: Partial saves and refused lines when a catalog product changed after the dish was saved.
  constraints:
  - id: ADR-018/C-005
    statement: A dish logged by products is sent as separate addLogEntry product entries; from_dish_id is never set.
    kind: interface
    check: review
  - id: ADR-018/C-006
    statement: The client keeps the lines together by sending them one after the other and shows the refused ones.
    kind: interface
    check: review
  affects:
  - CMP-001
  - IF-001
  consequences: The entry table needs no from_dish_id. Dishes with an unselectable product cannot be logged by products.
  cites:
  - REQ-014/AC-005
  - ADR-014/C-001
- key: c
  summary: A dish entry keeps the dish id and holds per-line amount overrides in a child table entry_lines. The entry stays
    one row and a dish with default amounts has no child rows.
  satisfies:
  - REQ-014/AC-004
  - REQ-014/AC-010
  pros: The entry still says which dish it was. One entry per eaten dish.
  cons: A second table and a second entry shape, with a totals function that must read overrides. Changing or removing a single
    product is a different operation from the product entries of REQ-014/AC-005.
  cost: A larger migration and a more complex totals read. Build effort not stated (A-034).
  reversibility: costly
  risks: Totals read the wrong amounts when overrides are missed. Harder to keep the day immutable for closing.
  constraints:
  - id: ADR-018/C-007
    statement: Migration 007 also adds entry_lines (entry_id, position, grams or pieces), written only with their entry; a
      dish entry's weight is the sum of its lines with the overrides applied.
    kind: data
    check: review
  - id: ADR-018/C-008
    statement: Totals for a dish entry read entry_lines first and dish_lines for positions without an override, both by exact
      product id.
    kind: data
    check: review
  affects:
  - CMP-002
  - CMP-001
  - IF-001
  consequences: IF-001 entries carry a lines list for dish entries.
  cites:
  - REQ-014/AC-010
  - A-034
recommended:
  option: a
  reason: It logs a dish with changed amounts in one transaction, keeps the dish's exact product ids even when a product was
    replaced or deleted (R-009, ADR-014), and leaves the catalog dish untouched. Option b fails for such dishes. Option c
    adds a second entry shape no AC needs.
