context:
  text: R-009 says a saved dish keeps pointing at the old product when that product gets new nutrient values (AC-015). After
    a nutrient or piece_weight edit the product has replaced_by set and is not selectable (ADR-010, ADR-011 option b). A product
    can also be soft-deleted (R-006), after which it is not selectable. No source says what a dish does then. The ticket asks
    whether a line may point at a deleted or replaced product and how the response marks it. Product ids are never reused
    and rows never deleted (ADR-010/C-002), so such a line always resolves.
  cites:
  - R-009
  - R-006
  - REQ-006/AC-015
  - ADR-010/C-002
  - ADR-011
  - Diet-Companion/diet-companion#92
options:
- key: a
  summary: A line keeps its exact product id for the life of the dish. The dish stays selectable. Each line returns its product
    in whatever state, with replaced_by, deleted and selectable, and the dish carries a derived has_unselectable_product flag.
    A new dish or a changed line must reference selectable products, and a line whose product id is already in the dish is
    carried over on edit without that check.
  satisfies:
  - REQ-006
  - REQ-006/AC-015
  - REQ-006/AC-018
  pros: Follows R-009 exactly and keeps repeat dishes usable after a product is corrected. The marking is data the client
    and later features can act on. No new rule about product deletion.
  cons: A dish can be selected while one of its products is deleted, so the journal and plans must decide what to do with
    the flag.
  cost: One derived flag and one check on new lines. Build effort not stated (A-008).
  reversibility: two-way
  risks: The Owner could plan a dish with a deleted product without noticing. Mitigated by the flag on the dish and on each
    line.
  constraints:
  - id: ADR-014/C-001
    statement: createDish and editDish accept a new or changed line only for a selectable product (error dish.line_invalid,
      reason product_not_selectable or product_not_found). On edit, a line whose product id is already in the dish is carried
      over without that check. A dish with a replaced or deleted product line stays selectable.
    kind: interface
    check: review
  - id: ADR-014/C-002
    statement: Every dish response returns each line's product in any state with replaced_by, deleted and selectable, and
      the dish carries has_unselectable_product. A line's product is read by its exact id, so a product renamed in place shows
      its new name (R-005, AC-018).
    kind: interface
    check: review
  affects:
  - CMP-002
  - IF-001
  consequences: Plans and the journal receive a flag and decide their own handling; that is a later design.
  cites:
  - R-009
  - R-006
  - A-008
- key: b
  summary: A dish is selectable only while none of its lines points at a soft-deleted product. A line on a replaced product
    does not affect selectability. The deleted state of the product is shown on the line.
  satisfies:
  - REQ-006
  - REQ-006/AC-015
  pros: The Owner cannot plan or log a dish that uses a product they deleted. The rule matches R-006 for products.
  cons: Deleting a product silently removes dishes from the selectable list, and restoring the product brings them back. Selectability
    depends on another table.
  cost: A join in the shared selectable predicate. Build effort not stated (A-008).
  reversibility: two-way
  risks: A dish seems to vanish with no action on it. Slower list queries. Not stated in R-009 or R-010.
  constraints:
  - id: ADR-014/C-003
    statement: The shared dish selectable predicate also requires that no line's product has deleted_at set. listDishes with
      state=selectable and dish selection use it. getDish still returns the dish in any state and the response states why
      it is not selectable.
    kind: interface
    check: review
  affects:
  - CMP-002
  - IF-001
  consequences: Product deletion changes which dishes are selectable.
  cites:
  - R-006
  - ADR-010/C-003
  - A-008
recommended:
  option: a
  reason: R-009 says the dish keeps pointing at the old product and neither R-009 nor R-010 ties dish selectability to product
    state. Option a adds no new rule and exposes the state as data.
