context:
  text: R-004 says a change of nutrient values records a new product. R-005 says changing only name, brand, shop or portion
    example changes the product in place. Neither defines nutrient values. The product record (IF-001 1.1.0) has name, shop,
    brand, calories, protein, fat, carbohydrate, sugars, fibre, salt, portion_size, portion_calories and piece_weight, and
    piece_weight decides whether a product is counted by the piece (R-003). R-005 reads as the full list of in-place edits,
    but REQ-005 does not say so for piece_weight. Assumption A-021 reads portion example as portion_size and portion_calories
    together. The Owner must settle this because it decides which edits change past totals.
  cites:
  - R-004
  - R-005
  - R-003
  - REQ-005
  - architecture/system/interfaces/IF-001/openapi-1.1.0.json
  - A-021
options:
- key: a
  summary: Nutrient values are calories, protein, fat, carbohydrate, sugars, fibre and salt. A change to any records a new
    product. All other fields, including piece_weight, are edited in place.
  satisfies:
  - REQ-005
  pros: Matches the plain meaning of nutrient. Correcting a piece weight does not create a new product.
  cons: A piece weight change alters the grams behind past entries counted by the piece, and so changes history, which REQ-005
    exists to prevent. It also changes counted_by_piece on products already in use.
  cost: No extra build effort stated (A-008).
  reversibility: two-way
  risks: Past totals could shift after an in-place piece_weight edit.
  constraints:
  - id: ADR-011/C-001
    statement: calories, protein, fat, carbohydrate, sugars, fibre and salt are nutrient values and a change to any records
      a new product. name, brand, shop, portion_size, portion_calories and piece_weight are edited in place. The field list
      is defined in one place in CMP-002.
    kind: interface
    check: review
  affects:
  - CMP-002
  - IF-001
  consequences: History can change when a piece weight is corrected.
  cites:
  - R-004
  - R-005
  - R-003
- key: b
  summary: Everything except name, brand, shop, portion_size and portion_calories is a nutrient value, so a change to calories,
    protein, fat, carbohydrate, sugars, fibre, salt or piece_weight records a new product.
  satisfies:
  - REQ-005
  pros: Follows R-005 as the full list of in-place edits. Past records keep the grams they were logged with. Any future value
    field is versioned by default, which is the safe side for history.
  cons: Correcting a typo in piece weight creates a new product and drops the old one from the weekly shopping plan.
  cost: No extra build effort stated (A-008).
  reversibility: two-way
  risks: The Owner may find a new product for a piece weight fix surprising.
  constraints:
  - id: ADR-011/C-002
    statement: A change to calories, protein, fat, carbohydrate, sugars, fibre, salt or piece_weight records a new product.
      Only name, brand, shop, portion_size and portion_calories are edited in place. The field list is defined in one place
      in CMP-002.
    kind: interface
    check: review
  affects:
  - CMP-002
  - IF-001
  consequences: IF-001 1.2.0 is drafted on this option.
  cites:
  - R-004
  - R-005
  - R-003
- key: c
  summary: Only the three required values, calories, protein and fat, are nutrient values. Every other field is edited in
    place.
  satisfies:
  - REQ-005
  pros: Fewest new products. The three values are the ones the targets use.
  cons: Past day totals for carbohydrate, sugars, fibre and salt could change after an edit, and R-004 and the product core
    speak of nutrient values in general, not only these three.
  cost: No extra build effort stated (A-008).
  reversibility: two-way
  risks: History for optional nutrients is not protected.
  constraints:
  - id: ADR-011/C-003
    statement: A change to calories, protein or fat records a new product. All other fields are edited in place. The field
      list is defined in one place in CMP-002.
    kind: interface
    check: review
  affects:
  - CMP-002
  - IF-001
  consequences: Optional nutrient edits rewrite history.
  cites:
  - R-004
  - knowledge/product-core.md
recommended:
  option: b
  reason: R-005 lists the in-place fields with the word only, so every other field versions. This protects past records, which
    is the value REQ-005 states, and piece_weight changes the grams behind past entries.
