responsibilities: 'Serves IF-001. Checks the Owner password and issues the Owner session (ADR-004 d). Validates products (R-002),
  enforces name plus shop uniqueness among selectable products (R-003), records a new product version on a nutrient edit (R-004),
  edits other fields in place (R-005), soft-deletes and restores products (R-006), and serves any product by id in any state.
  Also saves dishes with lines of products (R-007), records a new dish on a change of products or amounts (R-008), keeps each
  line on its exact product id (R-009), soft-deletes and restores dishes and allows repeated names (R-010), and serves any
  dish by id in any state with computed line weights. Also validates and saves daily target ranges for calories, protein and
  fat one nutrient at a time (R-011), dates each save to today in the Owner time zone (ADR-016), and answers the range in
  force today or on a date (ADR-015). Also keeps the day plan and the meal log per day and meal (R-007, R-013; ADR-017): entries
  are a product with whole grams or pieces, or a whole dish, on the exact ids; confirms a meal as planned by copying; splits
  a dish into product entries (ADR-018); refuses changes outside the day window or on a closed day (ADR-019); computes day
  and meal totals on read for later features. Owns the catalog store and the Owner session rows, and calls CMP-003 for label
  photos. Keeps no photo after the response. Written as a single modular unit; other areas will add modules later.'
